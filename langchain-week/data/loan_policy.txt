LOAN POLICY (SAMPLE) Version 1.0

Section 1: Eligibility
1.1 Applicants must be between 21 and 60 years of age at the time of application.
1.2 Salaried applicants need a minimum monthly income of Rs 25,000. Self-employed applicants need a minimum annual income of Rs 4,00,000.
1.3 A credit score of 700 or above is required. Scores between 650 and 699 are referred to a credit officer.
1.4 Total monthly EMIs including the new loan must not exceed 50 percent of monthly income.

Section 2: Loan amounts and tenure
2.1 Personal loans range from Rs 50,000 to Rs 15,00,000.
2.2 Tenure ranges from 12 to 60 months.
2.3 Loans above Rs 10,00,000 require senior manager approval.

Section 3: Interest and charges
3.1 Interest rates range from 10.5 percent to 16 percent per year, based on credit score.
3.2 Processing fee is 1.5 percent of the loan amount plus applicable taxes.
3.3 Prepayment is allowed after 6 months. A charge of 2 percent applies to the outstanding principal if prepaid within 12 months.
3.4 A late payment fee of 2 percent per month applies to overdue EMI amounts.

Section 4: Documents required
4.1 Identity proof: Aadhaar or passport.
4.2 Address proof: utility bill not older than 3 months, or rental agreement.
4.3 Income proof: last 3 salary slips and 6 months of bank statements for salaried applicants; last 2 years of ITR for self-employed applicants.

Section 5: Disbursal
5.1 Approved loans are disbursed within 3 working days after documents are verified.
5.2 Disbursal is made only to the applicant's own bank account.
